The RFT dataset

The report says a refined task-balancing strategy raised its score. We can check this without a GPU, because the dataset is public.

Source: delinqu/comet-1.5k, file meta/episodes.jsonl. 1469 episodes, 39 tasks.

The claim

Stage Q-score Source
Pre-training 0.192 Table 2
Post-training, at the challenge 0.224 Section 4.3
Post-training, refined 0.345 Table 2
Theoretical best 0.611 Table 2

The method keeps 1469 trajectories out of about 25,500 rollouts. That is 3 rounds of about 8500 each. If balancing raised the score, the kept set must be more even than the raw success distribution.

Finding 1: the balancing is a cap

Episodes kept per task, sorted:

Five tasks stop at exactly 120. The method caps the common tasks. It does not raise the rare ones.

The set is still very uneven after balancing.

Finding 2: short tasks give more data

Spearman rank correlation between episodes kept and episode length: -0.45.

Episodes kept Tasks Mean length
100 or more 8 7,457 frames (4.1 min)
10 to 99 15 16,563 frames (9.2 min)
Fewer than 10 16 16,212 frames (9.0 min)

Rejection sampling collects almost all of its data from the short half of the benchmark.

Finding 3: eleven tasks gave nothing

The dataset holds 39 tasks. The benchmark has 50. About 25,500 rollouts produced no kept episode for the other 11.

The method is silent where the benchmark is hardest. It cannot close the gap to the theoretical best of 0.611.

Finding 4: the native resolution is 720 and 480

The dataset records the head camera at 720x720 and the wrist cameras at 480x480. These are the values in row #4 of Table 3.

Image resolution Report
Head 224, wrist 224 0.30
Head 720, wrist 480 0.60

So the gain does not come from added information. It comes from not discarding information. The 224x224 setting downsamples data that is native at 720 and 480.

Finding 5: a stale frame count

info.json gives total_frames as 12,226,350. The sum of the episode lengths in episodes.jsonl is 14,896,614. The difference is 2.67 million frames, or 18%.

The count was likely not rebuilt after the last filter step. This is a note, not a problem for the results.

What this means

The report credits task balancing. The dataset shows the balancing does little. The distribution stays very uneven.

The real pattern is a bias by task length. Rejection sampling works where episodes are short. This is a sharper limit than the report states.
